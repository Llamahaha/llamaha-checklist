Submit a support ticket

Use this form when you need help reviewed or want to report a missing, unclear, or incorrect page. The form gathers the details that make the request easier to understand and sends them to admin@llamaha.com.

What helps the review go faster

Describe the request

Include the app or page, what you were trying to do, what changed recently, and the exact message or symptom you saw.

Add what narrows it down

Share the work account used, the app version when you can see it, screenshot details, whether the browser version works, and whether the same task works from another device, Cloud PC, Citrix session, or VPN path.

Point to the exact item

Include the exact file, project, mailbox, printer, site URL, shared path, data source, company file, phone prompt, guide URL, or guide section involved.
