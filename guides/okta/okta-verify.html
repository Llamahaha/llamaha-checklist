Application Guide

Overview

Okta Verify is the second-factor app many companies pair with Okta for push approvals, one-time passcode codes, and (with FastPass) device-based sign-in.

Most Okta Verify tickets are 'I got a new phone' and the fix is an Okta support team factor reset plus a fresh setup link.

Plain English

What this app is usually used for

Mobile multi-factor authentication app used for Okta-protected sign-ins including push notifications, number matching, and verified push.

It is part of the Okta family covered in our app help.

Common problems

Common problems

Got a new phone and Okta Verify isn't working

You have a new phone and Okta will not let you sign in.

Likely fix

Contact IT to verify your identity, then either use your firm's self-service factor reset or have IT factor-reset Okta Verify and send a fresh setup link.

What to collect

Send your name, the new phone model, and the apps you are blocked from.

Okta pushes are not arriving

Okta Verify is enrolled but no push arrives.

Likely fix

Check notification permissions for Okta Verify in phone settings, confirm internet connectivity, and refresh the app.

What to collect

Send your phone OS, the Okta Verify version, and whether notifications are enabled.

FastPass enrollment is failing

Your firm uses FastPass but enrollment will not complete or your device is not trusted.

Likely fix

Confirm Okta Verify is current and that any company device management or device-registration step your firm requires is completed.

What to collect

Send your phone OS, Okta Verify version, and whether your phone is enrolled in your firm's company device management.

First things to try

First things to try

Quick checks before you change anything

  • Is this a new enrollment, a phone replacement, a reactivation after reinstalling, or a failing push?
  • Does your company use Okta FastPass, or just push and one-time passcode?
  • Do you still have your old phone, and does it still receive Okta pushes?
  • Which apps are you trying to reach when Okta blocks you?

Try these fixes first

  • If you still have the old phone, keep it nearby — IT may be able to verify you with a push to it.
  • Check that notifications are enabled for Okta Verify in your phone settings.
  • If your firm offers a self-service Okta dashboard, try the factor reset there before contacting IT.

Slow down

Do not do this yet / warnings

Avoid these until support says it is safe

  • Before removing Okta Verify, confirm another approved factor exists, and have support reset the factor in Okta if the phone is being replaced.

What support needs

What details support needs

Send these details

  • A note about whether you still have the old phone.
  • Your phone model and OS version.
  • Whether notifications for Okta Verify are enabled in phone settings.
  • A screenshot of the Okta prompt or error, if you can take one.

Licensing & access

Licensing / access notes

Licensing / access checks

  • Okta Verify is free to install — your access depends on your company's Okta organization and your enrollment.
  • If you are switching phones, IT must verify your identity before resetting Okta factors.
  • FastPass requires the Okta Verify app plus any device registration steps your firm requires (sometimes through company device management).

More checks

More setup checks

Install / update basics

  • Install Okta Verify from the App Store or Google Play.
  • When IT sends you an Okta setup link, open it on the new phone and follow the prompts to add your work account.
  • Allow notifications so push approvals can reach you.

More things to check

  • Verify identity independently (HR, manager) before resetting Okta factors.
  • Prefer factor reset over user reset — a user reset breaks other Okta apps unnecessarily.
  • If FastPass is used, confirm whether device registration also needs re-enrollment, not just the factor.
