Application Guide

Overview

DocuSign is the electronic-signature service used to send agreements for signature, route them between signers, and store the signed copy.

Most DocuSign tickets are wrong signer email addresses, mail-flow filtering, or being signed into a personal DocuSign account instead of the company organization.

Plain English

What this app is usually used for

Browser-based e-signature workflow used to send envelopes, use templates, track routing, and have recipients sign.

It is part of the DocuSign family covered in our app help.

Common problems

Common problems

The signer says they didn't get the email

You sent an envelope but the signer reports nothing arrived.

Likely fix

Open the envelope on the Manage page, confirm the signer email is correct, then either resend or share the signing URL directly instead of recreating the envelope.

What to collect

Send the envelope ID, the signer email, and a screenshot of the recipient status on the Manage page.

The template is sending wrong fields or wrong signer order

Envelopes go out, but the fields or routing do not match what the template should do.

Likely fix

Open the template, confirm signer order and field assignments, and send a fresh test before changing in-flight envelopes.

What to collect

Send the template name, the envelope ID, and screenshots of both the template and the sent envelope.

I'm signed into the wrong DocuSign account

DocuSign opens but lands in a personal account or the wrong company organization.

Likely fix

Sign out completely, then sign in again using your firm's DocuSign company sign-in link or vanity URL.

What to collect

Send the account email shown, the company DocuSign URL you should use, and the company sign-in method.

First things to try

First things to try

Quick checks before you change anything

  • Are you the sender, a signer, or an support team or template owner?
  • What is the exact envelope name and what does the Manage page say about its status?
  • Which email address is DocuSign delivering to, and is that mailbox actually monitored?
  • Did the issue start after an company sign-in change, an email alias change, or a template update?

Try these fixes first

  • Always check the envelope status on the Manage page before resending or recreating an envelope.
  • Confirm the signer's email address is a real mailbox the signer actually monitors.
  • If you are sending from the wrong account, sign out completely before signing back in via your firm's company sign-in link.

Slow down

Do not do this yet / warnings

Avoid these until support says it is safe

  • Offboarding means transferring envelopes, templates, and shared folders to another user, then removing the user from the DocuSign account before revoking sign-in.

What support needs

What details support needs

Send these details

  • A screenshot of the envelope Manage page or recipient status.
  • The DocuSign account email shown when you are signed in.
  • The envelope ID and the signer email involved.
  • Your firm's DocuSign company sign-in link or vanity URL, if you have it.

Licensing & access

Licensing / access notes

Licensing / access checks

  • DocuSign sender access depends on being added to your company's DocuSign account by an support team.
  • Personal DocuSign accounts cannot send work envelopes — you must be signed into the company organization via your firm's company sign-in link or vanity URL.
  • Distribution lists, shared mailboxes, and Outlook aliases often break delivery — the signer email should be a real, monitored mailbox.

More checks

More setup checks

Install / update basics

  • DocuSign runs in the browser; there is no required desktop install.
  • Sign in via your firm's DocuSign company sign-in link rather than a generic DocuSign URL so you land in the right account.
  • If you also use the DocuSign for Outlook add-in, install it from your Office 365 portal or the Microsoft AppSource.

More things to check

  • Pull the envelope history and recipient status from Manage before you resend or recreate the envelope.
  • Confirm the sender's DocuSign account, permission profile, and whether company sign-in is sending them into the expected organization.
  • Check the signer's junk, quarantine, and alias mail flow before assuming DocuSign did not send.
